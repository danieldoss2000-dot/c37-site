Checklist

Forget nothing

What people get wrong, what they forget, and what we could add. Skim and tick.

Checklist

updated 1 Oct 2026
In the planTo decide or drawIdeaMistake to avoid

Mistakes people regret

  • White or cream sofas · fixed: mushroom + leather
  • One ceiling light per room · plan three layers: ambient, task, accent
  • Too few sockets, in the wrong places
  • Open plan noise and cooking smells · ours has movable partitions
  • AC units placed last · they fix the walls
  • Glossy dark floors in a dusty city
  • Storage planned after the furniture
  • Trendy tiles everywhere · keep bold for small rooms
  • Rugs too small for the seating
  • Curtain tracks forgotten until the ceiling is done

Kitchen & pantry

  • Work triangle checked, walkways ≥ 1 m
  • Hood ducted outside
  • Hidden pantry door, appliance garage, chest-height ovens
  • Sockets every metre on the worktop + inside the pantry
  • Bin and recycling pull-out
  • Water filter tap, water point for the fridge
  • Gas point and shut-off
  • Second fridge/freezer in the pantry
  • Charging drawer for phones
  • Corridor coffee station behind pocket doors · upstairs
  • One family table, chairs tucked, stools under the overhang · in round 4

Bathrooms & laundry

  • Shattaf on every WC, wall-hung WCs
  • Lit niches, walk-in showers, tub for the kids
  • Exhaust fan in every bath without a window
  • Floor drain + water inlet + dryer vent in the laundry
  • Water heaters: one central or one per wet room
  • Backing boards for future grab bars
  • Heated towel rail in the master
  • Socket inside the vanity cupboard for hairdryer
  • Fold-down ironing board in the laundry

Bedrooms & kids

  • Blackout + sheer in every bedroom
  • Wardrobes to the ceiling
  • Sockets and USB both sides of every bed
  • Two-way switches: door and bedside
  • Stair rails, no horizontal bars · gates later
  • Washable paint, rounded corners, socket covers in kids rooms
  • Desk wall wired for a home office until the kids arrive
  • Night-light circuit along the corridor
  • Bunk-ready double room, window seat

Entrance, storage & outside

  • Coat cupboard, store, linen cupboard · more storage designed later
  • Shoe storage + bench at the door
  • Pram and bikes: a place for them · storage designed later
  • Hose taps front and back, outdoor sockets on all sides
  • Garden lighting and irrigation points
  • Mosquito screens on opening windows
  • Water tank, AC units and gas on the side elevations
  • Doorbell camera, parcel shelf
  • Garden shed for bulk items

Light, power & tech

  • Lighting plan: ambient, task, accent, dimmers
  • Cove light strips and pendants on separate circuits
  • Sensor lights in pantry, store, dressing, corridor
  • Router in the middle of the house, ethernet to each TV
  • Empty conduits for later cables
  • AC outdoor units on the side walls, drains to follow
  • Smart thermostat and blind motors
  • Floor socket under the great-room seating
  • CCTV conduits to the four corners
