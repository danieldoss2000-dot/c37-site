H6-1 H6 · iteration 1 — wet wall west

The idea
  • Up the stair, the landing runs straight to the kids wing. The chapel door is on your right after two steps; the kids-wing door is at the far end, 1.6 m further on and facing the other way, so the two never meet.
  • The master shower room now fills the whole strip between hers and the chapel: long 1.7 m walk-in shower under the side window, WC on W1, double basin, towel shelf. The suite hall is one straight run from the bedroom to its door.

  • The kids lobby is a small square-cornered room with four doors: wing door, double room, play room and bath. No angled bite: the double room is a clean 4 × 4 m box with a 2 m wardrobe wall.

What changed vs H6, and why
  • Master shower room 5.7 → 7.1 m²: the round foyer and the suite-hall tail are gone (Daniel's red mark-up); the room takes their place, so the suite hall stops at the shower-room door instead of running past it.

  • Prayer room 4.9 → 3.3 m²: still an inner room, now a plain 1.9 × 1.95 m rectangle (no apse) with a bench for four facing the icon wall on the east. Its door is in the landing's side wall near the stair, away from the kids door (yellow mark-up).
